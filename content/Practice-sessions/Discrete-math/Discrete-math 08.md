# Discrete-math 8
## Dirichlet's function
$$
\displaylines{
D: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}, D(x) = \begin{cases}
1 & x \in \mathbb{Q} \\
0 & \text{otherwise} \\
\end{cases} \\
D[\mathbb{N}] = \Set{ 1 } \\
D^{-1}[\mathbb{N}] = \Set{ 1 } \\
D^{-1}[\mathbb{Z}] = \Set{ 0, 1 } \\
(a, b) = \Set{ x \in \mathbb{R}: a < x < b} \\
D^{-1}[(0, 1)] = \emptyset \\
D[(0, 1)] = \Set{ 0, 1 } \\
}
$$
