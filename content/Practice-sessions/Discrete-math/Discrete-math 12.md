# Discrete-math 12
$$
\displaylines{
\sum_{i=0}^{k} \binom{n}{i}\binom{n-i}{2k-2i}2^{2k-2i} = \binom{2n}{2k} \\
}
$$
$$
\displaylines{
n \leq m \\
\sum_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^{k}\binom{n+1}{k}\binom{m+1+n-k}{n-k} = \binom{m}{n} \\
}
$$
