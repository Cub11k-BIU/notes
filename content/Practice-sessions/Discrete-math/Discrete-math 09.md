---
$$
\displaylines{
\text{How many four digit numbers there are, comprised of digits } 1, 2, 3, 4, 5? \\
\text{Solution:} \\
5^{4} \\
\\
\text{How many such numbers with at least one } 3 \text{ are there?} \\
\text{Let us count the number of numbers without 3} \\
4^{4} \\
\implies \text{Number of numbers with at least one three is } 5^{4} - 4^{4} \\
}
$$
---
$$
\displaylines{
\text{How many ways to place } 2 \text{ black rooks on a chess board} \\
\text{such that they don't threaten each other?} \\
\text{Solution:} \\
\frac{64*(64-15)}{2!} = \frac{64*49}{2} = 32*49 \\
}
$$
